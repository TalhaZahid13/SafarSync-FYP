# THE SAFARSYNC STORY

## Background
Three friends - Ahmed (M), Fatima (F), and Ali (M) - plan a week-long adventure to Hunza Valley. 

**Scenario Challenges:**
- First time group trip with safety concerns
- Extreme weather in mountain region
- No prior travel together
- Family safety worries (especially for Fatima as solo female)
- Budget management & cost splitting
- Real-time communication & tracking

**SafarSync Value Proposition:**
SafarSync solves these with one integrated platform combining travel planning, safety monitoring, AI assistance, and mental health support.
